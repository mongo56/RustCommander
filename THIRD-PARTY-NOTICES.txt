UnRAR (reading RAR archives)
----------------------------

RustCommander reads RAR archives with RARLAB's UnRAR library (version 7.1),
compiled into the program from the C++ source shipped with the `unrar_sys`
crate (https://github.com/muja/unrar.rs, bindings licensed MIT OR
Apache-2.0). UnRAR is not open source: it is freeware under the license
below. It is used here only to read (extract) RAR archives; RustCommander
does not create RAR archives.
